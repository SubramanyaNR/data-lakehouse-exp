Our Story

Every Restaurant Tells a Story

Udupi veg restaurants are famous, pure vegetarian eateries known for authentic South Indian staples like dosa, idli, and vadas, originating from the Udupi Krishna Temple, serving traditional, fresh, and often diverse Indian vegetarian food, found globally and adapting modern tastes while maintaining purity.

Special Dish

Bisi-bele-bath

Savor the authentic taste of Karnataka with our Bisi Bele Bath, a rich and aromatic one-pot meal. This dish features a delightful medley of rice, toor dal (lentils), and mixed seasonal vegetables slow-cooked in a tangy tamarind extract and jaggery-based gravy, infused with our special, house-blended spices. Served piping hot, it's a complete and satisfying experience.
